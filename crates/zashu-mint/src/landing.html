Standard Cashu (BDHKE)

  • Speed: Sub-50ms, stateless bearer tokens.
  • Amounts: Power-of-2 splits (128+8+1) leak approximate size.
  • Policies: NUT-10/11 P2PK & HTLCs visible to the mint.
  • Offline Receive: Receiver must race online to /v1/swap.
  • Solvency: Users trust the mint not to run fractional reserve.

What Zashu Adds to Cashu

  • Verifiable Solvency (NUTZ-1): Drop-in sidecar proving liabilities ≤ L1 reserves for all Cashu + Zashu tokens.
  • Arbitrary Amounts (NUTZ-2): Hidden values in 1 Pedersen commitment—no power-of-2 splitting.
  • Offline-Safe Stealth (NUTZ-3): Double-spend-proof zsp1... receives + oblivious witness sync.
  • Hidden Predicates (NUTZ-4): Private P2PK, Multisig, Timelocks & Cross-Mint Atomic Swaps.

How Zashu Works Under the Hood

Four clean cryptographic layers combining Ragu non-uniform PCD folding, Tachyon prunable accumulators, and Halo 2 (Pasta/IPA, no trusted setup).

LAYER 1 · NUTZ-1 SOLVENCY

Folded Mint Accounting

Every mint, swap, and melt updates total_liabilities and folds into one 16 KB proof verified against Bitcoin Taproot UTXOs in ~1 ms.

LAYER 2 · NUTZ-2 UNIFIED k=13

Single-Proof Note Swaps

One Halo 2 proof enforces 32-level Poseidon Merkle membership, 64-bit range checks, and Pedersen value balance without revealing amounts.

LAYER 3 · TACHYON PRUNING

Bounded Mint Storage

Indexed nullifier accumulators block double-spends while pruning old epochs (~49.6% node reduction over 10k spends).

LAYER 4 · NUTZ-3 & 4

Oblivious Sync & Predicates

Wallets refresh Merkle witnesses via GET /v1/zk/sync without leaking note indices, and run hidden HTLCs across mints.

How Zero-Knowledge Works (In 3 Steps)

  • 1. Hide with Commitments: Your note's exact amount (137 sats), owner key, and spending policy are locked inside a Pedersen commitment + Poseidon hash—binding mathematically, invisible to the mint.
  • 2. Prove Rules Without Data: Spending creates one compact Halo 2 (Pasta/IPA) proof proving: (a) your note is in the mint's Merkle tree, (b) inputs equal outputs with 64-bit overflow protection, and (c) you satisfy the hidden policy.
  • 3. Fold & Prune (Ragu + Tachyon): The mint recursively folds hundreds of transitions into one ~16 KB NUTZ-1 proof, while Tachyon accumulators prune old nullifiers so storage stays flat.

Why It Runs Great on Mobile Phones

  • Hybrid BDHKE + ZK: Everyday $2 coffee taps still use Cashu's <50 ms BDHKE blind signatures. Phones only run ZK when shielding savings, sending offline-safe stealth notes, or locking private HTLCs.
  • Tiny Modular Circuits (Ragu): No bloated zkVM—each action runs only the minimal subcircuit it needs (k=8 for minting/folding, k=13 for unified swaps), verifying in ~1 ms.
  • No 100 MB Proving Keys: Halo 2 on Pasta curves requires no trusted setup, eliminating giant ceremony downloads on mobile apps.
  • Oblivious Sync (Zero Full-Chain Scan): Unlike legacy privacy wallets that trial-decrypt every transaction on the network, GET /v1/zk/sync pulls compact epoch deltas—battery & data scale with your activity, not global volume.
